The skin oracle reads every imported `HeroSkin` surface, actual vertex bone indices/weights, imported inverse bind transforms, and the completed Skeleton3D global bone poses. It does not reproduce the production sync helper. Multiple surfaces with the same anatomical material prefix are merged, with index offsets. The sum of oracle region vertices must equal the independently summed imported arrays: **8454** Godot vertex entries. The earlier5973 suggestion was not the actual imported array count; glTF surface/normal/UV splits can differ from authored unique vertices.

All measured candidates retain zero immediate all-vertex discontinuity, zero initial body-frame/anchor capture error, zero first-render discontinuity after interpolation reset, finite transforms/velocities, all hats, the separate board's extra momentum, independent damage regions, and healthy snapshot replacement. Damage/display callbacks in the non-live direct fixture cannot change the actual game's payout.

For every pin, immutable local pivots come from `PhysicsServer3D.pin_joint_get_local_a/b`. Their definitions are checked against the originally captured `bail_frames`, then current world body transforms measure the two physical pivot positions. A cone candidate uses the originally captured world pivot and corresponding original local frames. The acceptance bound remains **.05m**; it has never been relaxed.

Visible crossings use actual imported CPU-skinned region triangles at independently detected coarse-box overlap maxima. Proper edge-through-face intersections reject endpoint/shared-edge hits below .0001m. A spatial cell broad phase retains every intersecting triangle AABB; the exact segment/triangle narrow phase remains the oracle. This measures a selected worst coarse-overlap frame per pair, not a proof that no crossing occurs at every animation instant. Full snapshots and triangle/point examples are exported per case.

The final policy addresses two measured causes. GodotPhysics contact depenetration uses a biased velocity channel while pins solve ordinary velocity, so independent clamp changes and extra joint-only priority passes can separate constraint correction from contact recovery. A .3 anatomical shape bias reduces split recovery, with .8 on the torso for stronger self separation.32 coupled contact/joint passes retain the original90Hz simulation. The exact engine solver is the installed `ed1daf0bf` source ([body pairs](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/ed1daf0bf/modules/godot_physics_3d/godot_body_pair_3d.cpp), [pin solver](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/ed1daf0bf/modules/godot_physics_3d/joints/godot_pin_joint_3d.cpp), [step solver](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/ed1daf0bf/modules/godot_physics_3d/godot_step_3d.cpp)).

The second cause was the initial forearm exception waiting for complete separation of broad boxes. The shirt and forearm imported triangles initially do not cross, but the box corners overlap. The rounded/tapered chest removes most false corner volume; activating only the two forearm/torso self-contact pairs immediately prevents an inward fold while retaining the initial body/skin pose exactly. Head/pelvis uses its separate initially-overlapping query gate, and adjacent linked bodies retain their original exclusions.

`scripts/rider_collision.gd` is the shared shape factory used by `Skater` and `SkatingContacts`, eliminating physical/query drift. Torso uses16 points: eight XZ ellipse samples at localY=-.35 with radii(.24,.18), eight at Y=.35 with radii(.34,.22); collider offset is +.04Y. Forearms use(.28,.44,.30) boxes with -.03Y offsets. Other contact geometry remains unchanged. Nonuniform elliptical taper produces triangulated side faces; the independent initial-overlap SAT therefore enumerates every supporting plane from point triples and every point-pair direction crossed with box axes, including all true convex face/edge axes.

Shin COM is +.24Y with inertia from actual imported lower-leg bounds(.311,.512,.405); no visual/body frame is moved. The contact velocity oracle subtracts the actual world-axis COM offset. Connected anatomy receives one common affine energy correction, including a final vector-norm recheck after Y translation. The exact same correction transforms previous linear/angular histories so the ceiling cannot create a subsequent insurance injury. The board retains its independent limits and launch momentum. Analytical counterexamples exercise the real production operator, including the former(36,0,0)+(0,9,0)37.108m/s escape.
