
## Post-integration recovery continuation repair

A read-only review after the first accepted R02/R10 runs found a missing continuation path. A chosen backup recovery could preserve the corrupt original, then fail before replacement. Ordinary preference or new-claim commits used the unacknowledged durable snapshot as their contribution baseline instead of the explicitly selected recovery baseline. For a backed-up, already-funded 600000 profile this incorrectly credited restored funds and emitted a new-funded event. `commit` now retains `_recovery_baseline` until recovery is acknowledged, matching `retry`.

The new service cases reproduced this defect before the fix: RECORDS332 with2 failures at `builds/repair/R02/recovery-before/output.log.result.json`. The exact previous service source is retained beside that result. With the fix, RECORDS332/0 passed at `builds/repair/R02/recovery-fixed/output.log.result.json`. The preference continuation emits no contribution; the new claim contributes only42 above restored600000 and emits no new-funded event. Both independently reopen the committed record, retain optional historical values and expected preferences, verify exact backup/quarantine bytes, and verify repeated retries emit nothing.

The integrated review also found that `main.save_records` refreshed campaign labels but not the RESULTS report after a preference action successfully saved an earlier pending claim. It now refreshes `show_results` in RESULTS, matching explicit retry/recovery. Actual parsed M from a failed400000→600000 result now updates the banked amount, removes pending copy/action, and shows the acknowledged ending once.

Final expanded RECORDS_UI1400/0 passed at `builds/repair/R02/recovery-ui/output.log.result.json`, including actual M after a selected600000 backup recovery failure, an actual controller claim continuation earning42, and the pending RESULTS preference commit. `records_ui_report.json` retains the observed contribution/goal values, request counts, and existing30 headless layout cases. These are runtime tests with bounded commands and the exact certificate diagnostic exemption; native acceptance and the separately blocked strict editor import remain unchanged.
