# R10 — Truthful claims, recovery, and viewport UI

The HUD now shows the current claim, its improvement above the prior durable route best, and the banked treatment fund separately. A failed save leaves the banked meter unchanged and presents persistent `SESSION BEST / SAVE PENDING` and `UNBANKED +$…` copy. Pending preferences remain active and visibly unsaved. Retry Save is available in MENU, READY, and RESULTS, with main's existing `records` phase/modal guard.

The per-claim baseline is captured by main before settlement in `last_claim_improvement`. Fund totals come from RecordsStore; HUD does not sum route arrays. `last_goal_crossed` is the acknowledged durable contribution event used for the new funded ending. A retry that commits nothing produces no repeated money or ending event. The pending amount is the difference between the service's session and durable totals and may include an earlier stronger claim or another route.

Future-version saves display `NEWER SAVE PROTECTED` and offer no overwrite/recovery button. Corrupt saves with a validated backup offer only Restore Backup; without a backup they offer an explicit Start Fresh Save. The panel explains session-only play and preservation of the damaged original. Successful recovery acknowledges `BACKUP RESTORED / SAVED` or `FRESH SAVE / SAVED`. If preservation succeeds but write/readback fails, `BACKUP RECOVERY / SAVE PENDING` or `FRESH RECOVERY / SAVE PENDING` retains the chosen recovery and focuses Retry Save until acknowledgment; it never asks for another reset choice. Recovery choices use actual HUD signals and existing modal ownership, not a second modal state machine or a developer approval prompt. HELP restores a valid recovery control; completion restores ordinary visible focus. The visible phase controls and save action have explicit cyclic Tab/Shift-Tab order.

The R06 replay HUD reads injury severities from `replay_sample.limbs`, never the live ragdoll. It also accepts pure historical receipt presentation in REPLAY with a `RECORDED RECEIPTS` heading; main owns immutable event timing and must not score or play receipt audio during playback.
