## Why headless and Blender evidence are insufficient

The original deliverable had Blender renders of actual runtime geometry and HUD rectangles.
Those renders cannot demonstrate Godot skin shaders, camera motion, text rendering, or frame pacing.
Headless focus probes establish specific dispatcher defects but cannot prove all native controls.
Waveform checks cannot prove correct native output or a coherent sound mix.
This task directly observes the extracted candidate app a player would launch.
Do not substitute source execution for candidate execution in the release evidence.
Use source execution only to isolate a candidate-only discrepancy.

## Evidence capture policy

Prefer actual native app screenshots or Godot viewport captures from a displayed process.
Label every image with build ID, route, phase, time rate, and native versus fallback provenance.
Capture short native screen recordings for movement/camera/interpolation judgments.
Still screenshots alone cannot establish smoothness or prevent a transient clipping event.
Use available computer-use tools through their documented entry points.
Do not assume a visible surface exists or fabricate screenshots if native services fail.
A renderer/device/window failure leaves the corresponding acceptance case pending.
Record the exact error and any safe diagnostic tried; no task may pretend that gate passed.
A Blender MCP render can supplement an asset investigation but does not complete this task.
No external messages or model invocations are required for the native check.

## Native route walkthrough

For each route, open the extracted app in a new isolated record fixture.
Verify the candidate build identity and the displayed first-run fund state.
Select all three poses and hats and observe readable continuous body/board silhouettes.
Charge, push, and steer the recommended line with real keyboard input.

Make a deliberate taxi near miss and a separate genuine obstacle strike.

Follow the physical traffic handoff at the road without an abrupt occluded camera jump.

Watch replay in recorded, chase, side, and overhead view, then retry and change route.
Close and restart the candidate with the same isolated fixture to verify records persist.

## Required interaction matrix

Open help over a focused MENU button and press Enter: the route must not start underneath it.
Open help/pause over a focused READY pose button and press Enter: the pose must not change.
Close the modal and verify valid restored focus and intended keyboard navigation.
Hold Tab after clicking a non-modal button in RUN: held slow motion works consistently.
Lose window focus while charging; return and release Space: no surprise launch.
Lose focus while steering/slow/aftertouch; return with released keys: no stuck action.
Pause/help in RUN, CRASH, and REPLAY; verify frozen simulation and coherent resume.
Mute during a traffic loop and an impact sound; both are inaudible afterward.
Return RESULTS→REPLAY immediately; no stale live wheel or engine loop remains.
Use keyboard and mouse for Retry Save in an intentionally blocked fixture.
Verify pending versus committed funding wording and restart survival after a successful retry.

## Independent pass criteria

All three native walkthroughs finish without a script/engine error or control dead end.
Wheel support and camera observations agree with upstream quantitative bounds.
Modal keyboard actions do not affect underlying gameplay.
There is no unexpected route/reset interpolation streak or persistent visible skin detachment.
Replay explains the same event order/damage and all four camera choices visibly differ.
Action audio follows the selected R11 policy and native mute works for every category.
Committed records survive a candidate restart; failed saving is plainly pending.
Native menu/results/help remain legible at every declared supported size actually tested.
Capture evidence from the extracted candidate, including its manifest identity.
A reviewer can connect observations to the input sequence and exact artifact tested.

This is stability/feedback validation, not a claim that the game is objectively fun.
If a real playtest participant is available, record their observed confusion/retry intent
without turning subjective notes into fabricated statistics or an enjoyment guarantee.
No human-response survey or new analytics service is required to close stability cases.

For each failure attach route/pose/control trace and a native recording or screenshot.

Reopen the owning repair task instead of lowering its acceptance requirement.
Any source or asset fix invalidates the candidate identity and requires a new R13 candidate.
Rerun affected native cases plus launch/replay/save smoke checks on all three routes.
Do not reuse screenshots from a replaced build as evidence for the new package.
If native execution is unavailable, retain candidate and explicit pending checklist;
R15 promotion stays blocked by this task, while independent source repairs remain completed.
