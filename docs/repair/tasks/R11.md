## Investigation rather than invented defect

`main.gd:501–526` returns from replay before updating rolling and traffic loops.
`main.gd:532–533` updates those loops only outside the replay branch.
This makes stale loop state plausible, particularly entering replay immediately after settlement.
Actual unwanted native audio has not been demonstrated.
Headless mode deliberately avoids native AudioStreamPlayer allocation in the sound director.
A test that checks zero players in headless mode cannot prove normal playback correctness.
Attempt source-process playback in isolated storage before editing. Separate native player-state
observations from listening. If native allocation is unavailable, change only independently
reproduced behavior and publish every unobserved native case as a mandatory R14 gate.

Why: sound communicates launch, injury, cascade handoffs, and claim settlement.
Lingering engines or repeated receipts can make frozen replay/results feel broken.
The repair scope is coherent playback state, not new composition or a large mixer framework.

## Chosen state policy

Keep the fifteen existing assets unless inspection finds clipping or a broken resource.
Maintain separate policies for music, continuous action loops, and event one-shots.
Music may continue through pause/help at the existing intended level.
Rolling and driver-engine loops stop or fade promptly on RESULTS, MENU, and REPLAY.
Paused gameplay loops pause coherently and resume without replaying old one-shots.
Mute affects every bus/player including currently playing pooled effects.
A muted preference applies immediately and remains session-active if persistence fails.
Focus loss uses the R03 pause policy; do not continue action audio while gameplay is suspended.
Replay uses music only in this repair; stop live action audio at entry and do not add an event track.
Visible receipts/events remain in R06; playback cannot call live scoring or sound callbacks.
A later replay event track would need its own recorded sound/pitch/gain schema and isolated cooldowns.
No deferred replay effect can play after playback returns to results.

## Timing and resource contract

Loop envelope duration uses active presentation time from R05.
Do not calculate envelope increments from a newly changed Engine.time_scale.
Stream pitch changes stay bounded and must not use a zero or negative pitch.
Use the existing pool or a small bounded pool; no player is allocated per physics contact.
Stop and detach streams during sound director teardown.
Retrying/changing routes does not leave a previous director or loop alive.
An exported app must load audio through ResourceLoader rather than assuming source WAV paths.
Native audio device failure is a recoverable lack of playback, not a game crash.
Report backend failure separately from a gameplay transition defect.

## Implementation sequence

1. Inspect current sound allocation, bus levels, pause modes, and teardown.
2. Launch an audio-enabled diagnostic in an isolated candidate/source sandbox.
3. Exercise immediate results-to-replay and observe actual loop state and audible behavior.

7. Enter/exit music-only replay explicitly without consuming live sound cooldowns or event callbacks.
8. Verify mute applies consistently to all allocated players and newly requested sounds.
9. Run the focused native state test, audio resource tests, and route-teardown test.
10. Hand audible quality/mix observations to R14 for final native validation.

## Independent acceptance cases

- Enter RUN, verify rolling sound starts at an eligible speed and stops after settlement.
- Enter RESULTS and immediately REPLAY; no live rolling/traffic engine remains audible.
- Pause during a cascade; no new impact/receipt one-shot is triggered while paused.
- Resume once; paused sounds do not restart as a burst of old events.
- Toggle mute during a long effect and during a loop; both stop audibly or mute via bus.
- Request a new effect while muted; it remains inaudible.
- Repeat retry and route replacement twenty times; allocated player count remains bounded.
- Play replay twice; music-only policy persists and no live impact/receipt/engine sounds fire.
- Exit replay early; no stale action sound can resume after results return.
- Verify invalid/empty audio resources fail cleanly without a game SCRIPT ERROR.
- Inspect audio files for nonempty length, finite samples, and peak clipping.
- Publish the source audio inventory for R13 to verify the exported candidate; no candidate blocks R11.

Native allocation/state tests are separate from listening evidence.
Waveform/peak checks can prove file properties but do not prove pleasant mixing.
Headless resource checks remain useful and should not be removed.
Do not insert fabricated sound-player mocks that merely mirror the phase method.
If the environment cannot open audio, record the exact backend limitation and leave native
acceptance pending rather than asserting the sound experience is verified.

## Risks and fallback

Pooled effects may still be playing when mute or replay starts; inspect every active player.
Repeated phase callbacks must not queue another finish/receipt sound.
Music-only replay is chosen to avoid an unnecessary event-track feature and live cooldown coupling.
Do not tie a live engine loop to a wreck that is UNCONTROLLED or SETTLED.
Changing sound pool lifetime can cause orphan warnings at exit; preserve explicit teardown.
Prefer a measured small transition fix over replacing the audio engine.
If the baseline transition works, keep it and attach the observation as a completed investigation.
