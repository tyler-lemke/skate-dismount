# Design cross-score: claims, cascades, and impact

Scores are out of 1000 for the stated goal: a slower, more legible Porrasturvat crash game with visible injuries, satisfying domino destruction, and a humane insurance-payout story.

| Proposal | Score | Strongest contribution | Remaining risk |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Impact: persistent limb damage, automatic crash slow motion, impact pulses, selectable poses | 930 | Makes the rider's physical hits readable and gives each launch a deliberate setup. | Slow motion must preserve normal run settlement and clear on pause, replay, and retry. |
| Cascade: branching lanes, tighter blasts, slower fuses | 905 | Turns large explosions into visible cause-and-effect domino runs with route choices. | If spacing and fuses are too conservative, runs may stall before a second cluster. |
| Claims story and interface: payout ledger, treatment fund, injury file, receipts | 895 | Gives each hit an understandable reward and the three routes an emotional reason to replay. | The fund goal must be calibrated against actual reachable payouts; UI text must stay legible during rapid hits. |

The strongest combined loop is: choose a pose and lane, launch, watch a readable injury and object cascade, then review exactly how much of the claim moved Mom's treatment fund. The goal is fictional and the insurer is the comic target. Mom's notes should sound like a real person who cares about the skater.
