# Codex rebuttal: motion, planted feet, and the dismount

I revise my earlier five. The stronger package is **I11, I03, I05, I01, I02**, with a tightly specified I09 handoff as an integration gate. This is my internal assessment, not a Grok response or playtest result.

| Idea | Earlier score | Revised score | Why it moved |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| I11 animation driver | 73 | **88** | I over-penalized the proposed seven-state/full-rig version and underweighted its credible first slice. `pose_ride()` sets bob and lean, then `apply_launch_pose()` replaces much of that pose every frame (`rider.gd:76–96`). One authoritative push/coast/carve driver fixes an existing visible defect and makes I03 and I05 legible. No new skeleton or airborne clip belongs in the first slice. |
| I09 dismount handoff | 91 | **76** | I treated handoff continuity as a missing feature. `bail()` already places each physical part at its visible global transform (`rider.gd:162–201`). New motion may expose a seam, but that is a validation gate before it is a standalone project. |
| I02 feet on deck | 81 | **82** | A planted foot is the spatial invariant that keeps I11's motion and I03's kick credible. Its first slice is stable deck anchors and a bent-knee stance; add a small two-segment solve only where fixed poses visibly slide. This is distinct from I11's temporal animation ownership. |

**Steelman of I11:** a skater can have a good motor and a better model yet still look weightless if the visible body does not answer push, coast, and turn events. The current pose overwrite makes that a present problem, not speculative polish. I11 should own one pose pipeline: contact cues and speed from I03, heading/lean from I05, stance constraints from I02, and a final pose that the existing ragdoll can consume. Its objection remains scope: a full skinned rig, seven clips, and new airborne states would bury the first playable test. Stop at push/contact/recovery, coast, and carve on the current segmented body.

**Why I02 keeps the fifth slot over standalone I09:** players can see the rear foot detach for a push and the front foot remain planted on every approach. That is a repeated, falsifiable improvement. I09's current transform copy already covers the central handoff claim. Preserve and test it as I11 changes output: the last rendered stance and joint anchors should match the first ragdoll frame; obstacle bail must fire immediately; manual bail must not add an unexplained delay; actual stair injuries and cascade payouts must remain attainable under the intentional .72/downward carry. If those checks fail, repair that specific seam rather than replacing the carry model.

Build the motion slice on Civic first: fix authoritative pose ownership, connect one W push and coast to it, add a bounded A/D carve, constrain the planted stance, and only then fit the I01 character to frozen pivots. Record ordinary gameplay framing, foot/deck error, time to first crash, and first-frame ragdoll continuity. Escalate I09 into a separate feature only if observed discontinuity survives that integration pass. This retains crash choreography as the payoff and avoids a skateboard simulator rewrite.
